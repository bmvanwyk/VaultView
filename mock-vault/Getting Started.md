# 🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to VaultView! Here's how to get the most out of it.

## Navigation
- **File tree** on the left — click any note to open it
- **Search** — type in the search bar to find anything
- **Graph** — click 🕸️ to see how notes connect

## Editing
- Click **✏️ Edit** to open the full-screen editor
- Use **Ctrl+S** to save, **Esc** to cancel
- Write in markdown with `[[wikilinks]]`

## Tips
- The **backlinks panel** shows which notes link here
- Notes auto-sync to git every hour (if configured)
- Works on mobile too!
